What the ledger tracks

Every bet type, logged the way sportsbooks actually price them. No spreadsheets, no guessing at your record two weeks into conference play.

SPR

Spreads

Log the line you took and the number that closed. Cover margins calculate themselves, push to push, dog to favorite.

ML

Moneylines

Straight-up bets get priced correctly whether you're laying -350 on a one seed or grabbing +260 on a mid-major upset pick.

O/U

Totals

Track overs and unders separately from side bets, with final combined scores pulled in automatically at tip-off's end.

u

Unit-based profit

Set your unit size once. Every wager, win, and loss gets converted so your record reads the same whether you bet $20 or $200 a game.

How it works

01

Log the bet

Enter the matchup, bet type, the line you got, and your stake in units. Takes about as long as reading the odds board at your book.

02

Track it live

Once the final buzzer sounds, results settle automatically. Your win, loss, or push posts to the ledger with the unit swing already calculated.

03

Spot the trend

Slice your record by team, conference, or bet type. Six weeks in you'll know if you're actually good on Big Ten unders or just think you are.
